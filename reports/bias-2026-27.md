# Why the models under-predict: 2026/27 to GW10

Issue #174. Every logged model predicts fewer points than players score. This report finds out where the missing points go, so they can be fixed in the model that will carry on: gbm-v1 is set to become the main model (ADR 0020).

This is a diagnosis, not evidence (ADR 0006). It reads the committed entries and the actuals they were scored against, and replays GW2–GW10 with `fpl-backtest`'s leak-free view. It changes no model and no entry.

## Summary

- **The models fall short for different reasons.** Attacking points are not the cause, and neither is a high-scoring season.
- **baseline-v1 and form-fixture-v1 under-predict rotation players.** Players they expect to play 1–29 minutes play nearly twice that and score more than twice the predicted points. The bands with fewer than 60 expected minutes hold all or nearly all of their shortfall. Their total minutes are about right: the models spread minutes too far toward the extremes. Neither model will change: the baseline is fixed (ADR 0007), and form-fixture-v1 is set to retire. gbm-v1 doesn't have this problem.
- **gbm-v1 under-predicts regular starters' defensive contributions.** The rule giving points for defensive contributions began in 2025/26. gbm-v1 trained on 2016/17–2025/26, so nine of its ten seasons have none of those points in their labels. It has no feature for them, and nothing to tell its seasons apart. Take defensive-contribution points out of the actuals, and its gap for players expected to play 60+ minutes goes from −0.28 to +0.02 points per player.
- **What to do:** stage 2 of #174, a gbm-v2 that predicts defensive contributions separately. See [What next](#what-next).

## Hypotheses

### 1. This season scores more than past ones: rejected

Points and components per 90 minutes, for players in the game now, from the snapshot's own past-season totals. The database was not running for this, so players who have left the league are not counted.

| Season | Minutes | Points | Goals | Assists | Bonus | Defensive contributions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023/24 | 418,546 | 3.92 | 0.157 | 0.142 | 0.316 | (not recorded) |
| 2024/25 | 515,134 | 3.87 | 0.143 | 0.124 | 0.313 | 6.95 |
| 2025/26 | 618,354 | 4.25 | 0.126 | 0.117 | 0.312 | 7.03 |
| 2026/27 (GW1–10) | 196,649 | 4.19 | 0.119 | 0.111 | 0.292 | 7.03 |

This season scores slightly less per 90 than last. The jump came in 2025/26, from 3.87 to 4.25 points per 90, the season defensive-contribution points began. Goals and assists per 90 have fallen. That jump comes back under hypothesis 6.

### 2. Shrinkage pulls attackers down: rejected

If the shortfall came from shrinking attackers' rates toward their priors, it would sit with expensive or in-form attackers. It sits with players who have had no goal involvements so far this season (committed entries):

| Model | 0 goals + assists | 1–2 | 3–5 | 6+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline-v1 | −568 | −49 | +24 | +15 |
| form-fixture-v1 | −505 | −89 | +22 | +9 |
| gbm-v1 | −28 | −73 | −27 | −8 |

Players priced at £10.0m or more are slightly *over*-predicted by every model.

### 3. Expected minutes are wrong: confirmed for baseline-v1 and form-fixture-v1

Committed entries, by expected minutes. Each figure is the predicted minus the actual total points in that band:

| Model | 0 | 1–29 | 30–59 | 60–79 | 80+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline-v1 | −87 | **−407** | −141 | +49 | +8 |
| form-fixture-v1 | −56 | **−369** | −126 | −7 | −6 |
| gbm-v1 | −11 | −16 | −8 | **−66** | −35 |

baseline-v1's 704 rows in the 1–29 band expected 7,713 minutes in total and got 13,430. 105 of those players played 60+ minutes, and scored 388 points against 65 predicted.

The GW2–GW10 replay (5,890 rows a model) shows how. The mean expected minutes are right overall (30.2 against 30.0 for both models), but too far out at each end:

| Expected minutes | Mean expected | Mean actual | baseline-v1 bias per player | form-fixture-v1 | gbm-v1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–29 | 11.2 | 16.7 | −0.46 | −0.49 | +0.02 |
| 30–59 | 45.1 | 43.7 | −0.17 | −0.22 | +0.02 |
| 60+ | 82.1 | 75.0 | +0.12 | +0.04 | **−0.28** |

The expected-minutes columns are baseline-v1's and form-fixture-v1's, which share a minutes model. In a replay every player counts as available, so the predictions run high for injured players. Read the bands against each other, not each one on its own.

gbm-v1's two-stage minutes model gets the rotation bands about right. Its gap is with regular starters, which is hypothesis 6.

### 4. One component is responsible: confirmed for gbm-v1, as hypothesis 6

The plan was to re-run form-fixture-v1 with its components kept apart. That wasn't needed: hypothesis 3 accounts for its shortfall, and the model is set to retire. gbm-v1 predicts total points, so its components can only be tested against the actuals. Hypothesis 6 does that.

### 5. New players: rejected

Players with no 2025/26 season account for 15% of baseline-v1's shortfall and 28% of its rows. They are, if anything, predicted better than everyone else. The other two models show the same.

### 6. Defensive contributions are missing from gbm-v1's training: confirmed

From the GW2–GW10 replay of gbm-v1, by position and expected minutes. "Bias without DC" takes each player's actual defensive-contribution points out before comparing:

| Position | Expected minutes | Rows | Predicted | Actual | DC points | Bias per player | Bias without DC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GKP | 60+ | 178 | 582 | 623 | 0 | −0.23 | −0.23 |
| DEF | 60+ | 668 | 1910 | 2209 | 338 | **−0.45** | +0.06 |
| MID | 60+ | 599 | 2024 | 2088 | 140 | −0.11 | +0.13 |
| FWD | 60+ | 132 | 501 | 543 | 0 | −0.32 | −0.32 |
| All | 60+ | 1577 | 5018 | 5463 | 478 | **−0.28** | +0.02 |
| All | <60 | 4313 | 2801 | 2702 | 164 | +0.02 | +0.06 |

Defensive contributions account for all of the regular defenders' gap, and more than all of the midfielders'. gbm-v1 picks up some of them for midfielders through its recent points-per-90 features. The same pattern shows in the committed GW9 and GW10 entries: regular defenders were 72 points short over 143 rows, and earned 84 points from defensive contributions.

Regular forwards (−0.32) and goalkeepers (−0.23) earn no defensive-contribution points, and stay short. With 132 and 178 rows, that's too few to tell from noise. Worth watching, not acting on yet.

## What next

Stage 2 of #174: **gbm-v2**, which predicts defensive contributions on their own, from the seasons that have them.

- **Points model:** the gbm-v1 recipe, trained on each match's points *minus* its defensive-contribution points, over all ten seasons. That target means the same thing in every season.
- **Defensive-contribution model:** expected defensive-contribution points per match, trained on 2025/26 alone. The database has the per-match counts (`player_fixture_stats.defensive_contribution`), and new features would give it the rate per 90 over recent windows.
- **Prediction:** the sum of the two.
- **Gate:** it has to beat gbm-v1 on the GW2–GW10 replay, and on held-out 2025/26, with regular starters' bias closer to zero in every position. It becomes the challenger once that slot is free, after GW11 settles gbm-v1's place (ADR 0020).

baseline-v1 and form-fixture-v1 stay as they are. This report is the record of why they under-predict.

## Method

Exploratory scripts in `scratch/` (gitignored), run against snapshot `20261110T235748Z`:

- Committed entries joined to `live/<gw>.json`, splitting points by `explain` identifier.
- Replays through `fpl.evaluation.backtest.replay`, GW2–GW10, every player marked available.
- Past seasons from each player's `history_past` in the same snapshot.
